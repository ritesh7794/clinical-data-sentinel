Clinical Data Sentinel

End-to-End PHI Detection, Analysis, Decision, and Compliance Orchestration

Architecture Model 4-Layer Security & Compliance Flow

Architecture Layers Explained

Layer 1: Input Sources

All data sources feeding into Clinical Data Sentinel: M365 mailboxes, webhook triggers, email events, email content, recipient information, organizational policies, and user context.

Layer 2: AI Detection & Analysis

The processing engine: LLM-based AI detection, 8 PHI pattern recognition, context analysis, risk scoring (ANOMALOUS/REVIEW/SAFE), confidence scoring (73-85%), compliance mapping (DPDP/NABH), and incident logging.

Layer 3: Admin Decision & Action

Human-in-the-loop control: Admin dashboard shows incidents, decision point determines action, three possible outcomes (Approve/Review/Block), and complete action logging for audit trail.

Layer 4: Output & Compliance

Deliverables for different stakeholders: Incident database (7-year retention), analytics dashboard (risk insights), compliance reports (DPDP/NABH proof), audit trail (regulatory ready), employee insights (training), governance portal (executive view), and external integrations.

Key Capabilities

🔍 Real-Time Detection

Scans every M365 email instantly for 8 PHI patterns with 87% accuracy. No delays, no missed emails.

⚡ Risk Classification

Classifies incidents as ANOMALOUS (85%), REVIEW (60%), or SAFE (73%) based on PHI patterns and context.

✓ Admin Control

Simple one-click decisions: Approve to send, Review for more context, or Block to prevent leak.

📋 Regulatory Compliance

Complete audit trail meets DPDP Act 2023 Article 8 and NABH Standard 2.3. Regulator-ready evidence.
